# NIST Alignment

This portfolio exercise uses NIST guidance as a structured reference, not as a claim of organizational certification or compliance.

## NIST CSF 2.0
| CSF Function | Application in Case Study |
|---|---|
| GOVERN | Investigation authority, policy, roles, Legal/HR coordination, risk decisions |
| IDENTIFY | Determine affected data, systems, users, business impact and dependencies |
| PROTECT | Least privilege, data handling, DLP, removable-media and cloud controls |
| DETECT | Identity, endpoint, file-access and DLP anomaly detection |
| RESPOND | Triage, investigation, evidence preservation, escalation, communications |
| RECOVER | Corrective actions, lessons learned and control improvement |

## NIST SP 800-61
Applied concepts include preparation, detection/analysis, containment considerations, eradication/recovery coordination, documentation, and post-incident learning.

## NIST SP 800-86
Applied concepts include integrating forensic techniques into incident response, preserving evidence integrity, documenting collection, and coordinating technical analysis with incident-handling needs.

## NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 — Relevant Control Families
- **AC — Access Control:** least privilege and access governance.
- **AU — Audit and Accountability:** event logging and investigative traceability.
- **IR — Incident Response:** investigation, reporting, handling and coordination.
- **MP — Media Protection:** removable-media governance.
- **SI — System and Information Integrity:** monitoring and detection.
- **PS — Personnel Security:** coordinated insider-risk considerations within authorized processes.

## Investigative Principle
Frameworks organize the response, but conclusions remain evidence-driven. A control mapping does not establish that misconduct occurred.
